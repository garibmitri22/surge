# First Three Conversations — landing customer #1

Goal: one paying managed customer + one documented outcome ("it rebooked X jobs"). That single proof turns on the moat and makes you investor-ready. This is an owner (Mitri) playbook.

## The principle
Don't sell software. Sell an outcome, and remove all risk: **"Let me run your old customer list and rebook some jobs for you — you approve every message, you owe nothing until you see results."** Reactivation first because it's the fastest, lowest-risk "wow" (revenue from a list they already own, no ad spend, fully legal).

## Who to approach, in order
1. **Warmest contractor with an existing customer/quote list** (roofing or HVAC — high ticket). Your strongest relationship who'll let you run a pilot. List = the raw material for the instant win.
2. **A second contractor** — a referral from #1, or another contact. Same reactivation pilot. Tests repeatability.
3. **One cold "active advertiser"** (from `outreach/active-advertiser-outreach.md`) — a home-services business running ads 4+ months. Tests the cold motion and the speed-to-lead pitch, not just warm relationships.

(The gym friend is a great demo but lower ticket — use for proof/testimonial, not the first revenue target.)

## The conversation arc (each one)
1. **Hook (their language):** "You're leaving money in your old quotes and past customers — people who already trust you. I built something that texts/emails them for you and rebooks jobs. Want me to show you on your actual list?"
2. **The risk-reversal:** you run it managed (you operate Surge), they approve every message, no charge until they see booked jobs. Removes "is this real / is it spammy" fear.
3. **The demo = the close:** upload their list → Aria drafts reactivation in their voice → they approve a batch → real replies/bookings come back → "that's $X in jobs from a list you forgot about."
4. **The offer:** Managed Growth Engine, $2,500–5,000/mo (`offers/managed-growth-engine-home-services.md`). Collect manually (invoice / Stripe payment link — no self-serve billing needed).

## What to capture from each (this is research, not just selling)
- Pricing reaction (what makes them flinch vs. nod) — your first 3 convos are a pricing experiment.
- Which proof lands hardest (the rebooking number? the speed? the "done for you"?).
- Objections + how you answered. Feed these back to Cowork → into the pitch + product.

## Definition of success
- 1 contractor says yes to a pilot.
- 1 real documented outcome (jobs rebooked) you can screenshot.
- Then: that becomes the testimonial + the investor proof, and the dogfooded engine gets pointed at filling their fresh pipeline too.

## Pre-reqs (don't start cold without these)
- Dogfood passed (`DOGFOOD-ACCEPTANCE.md`) — the engine works.
- For SMS reactivation: Twilio + 10DLC done. (Email reactivation works now without it — you can start there.)
