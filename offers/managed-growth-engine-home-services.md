# Surge — Managed Growth Engine (Home Services)
### Done-for-you lead generation for roofing, HVAC & flooring businesses

## The promise
**You show up to booked appointments. We run everything that gets you there.**
Your AI team finds the leads, answers every one in under two minutes, follows up relentlessly, and rebooks your old quotes — so you stop losing jobs to whoever called the homeowner back first.

## The problem we kill
In home services, **speed is the whole game.** The contractor who responds first wins the job — and most owners are on a roof, not by the phone. Every lead that waits 30 minutes is a $10,000–$30,000 job handed to a competitor. You're also sitting on a goldmine of old estimates that never closed and past customers who'd buy again — and no one has time to work them.

## What you get
- **A brand-new website, built free.** We design and build you a clean, fast site that makes your business look like the biggest operator in town — free. We host it, maintain it, and keep it updated as part of your plan. Every form and call button on it feeds straight to your AI team, so no lead ever sits unanswered.
- **Nova runs your ads.** Builds and manages your Meta + Google campaigns — creative, copy, targeting, optimization. Your growth engine's top of funnel, handled.
- **Aria answers every lead in <2 minutes, 24/7.** Texts the homeowner instantly, qualifies them, and books the appointment — then one-tap connects the call straight to you when you want it live.
- **Database reactivation.** We work your existing, consented list — old quotes and past customers — to pull booked jobs out of contacts you already have. Usually pays for the whole service in month one.
- **Weekly performance report.** A one-page Monday briefing: leads in, appointments booked, jobs in motion, and your Workforce Performance Score. You always know it's working.
- **Fully compliant by design.** Consent-native texting and calling (TCPA + 10DLC handled), so you grow without legal exposure.

## How it works
1. **Setup (week 1):** we build your new website, load your business, your services, your pricing, and your past-customer list, and stand up the ad campaigns.
2. **Engine on:** ads go live, leads start flowing in.
3. **Instant engagement:** every lead gets an immediate, on-brand response and a booked appointment — automatically.
4. **You close + we report:** you run the jobs; we send the weekly scoreboard and keep optimizing.

## Pricing
**Founding-customer rate — $1,500/month, locked for life.**
*(Standard rate will be $2,500–$5,000/mo. Founding customers keep $1,500 forever as we grow — you're betting on us early, so you lock the rate.)*
- **You pay nothing until you see booked jobs.** We point it at your existing list first; the retainer only starts once it's working.
- Month-to-month, cancel anytime. Ad budget (paid directly to Meta/Google) is added later, only when we turn on the ad channel.
- Floor: never below $997. This is a value rate, not a cheap one.

**Why it's a bargain:** one rebooked roofing/HVAC job ($10,000–$30,000) covers most of a year. A marketing agency runs $3,000–$8,000/mo and a single inside sales rep costs $4,000–$5,000/mo loaded — and you'd need both to do what this does.

## Who it's for
Residential roofing, HVAC, and flooring owners doing real volume who are losing leads to slow follow-up and leaving money in an unworked customer list.
