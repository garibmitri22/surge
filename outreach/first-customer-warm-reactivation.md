# Customer #1 — Warm Reactivation Pilot (owner playbook)

The fastest, lowest-risk path to first revenue: run a contractor's existing old-quote / past-customer
list and rebook jobs. Email reactivation needs no 10DLC, so it can start the moment the dogfood engine
is verified. Sell the OUTCOME, reverse all risk: "you approve every message, you owe nothing until you
see booked jobs."

## The conversation arc (the demo IS the close)
1. **Hook (their language):** "You're leaving money in old quotes and past customers — people who already
   trust you. I built something that texts/emails them for you and rebooks jobs. Let me show you on your
   actual list."
2. **Risk reversal:** you operate it (managed), they approve every message, no charge until booked jobs.
   Kills the "is this real / is it spammy" fear.
3. **Demo = close:** upload their list → Aria drafts reactivation in their voice → they approve a batch →
   real replies/bookings come back → "that's $X in jobs from a list you forgot about."
4. **Offer:** Founding-customer rate **$1,500/mo, locked for life** (standard rate becomes $2,500–5,000). **You pay nothing until qualified appointments are booked on your calendar.** White-glove: I personally set it up and tune it to your voice + list, approving alongside you, until it's reliably booking — then it runs hands-off. Month-to-month. Collect via invoice / Stripe link.
5. **Sweetener (the free-website hook, June 9):** "And I'll build you a brand-new website, free — hosted and maintained as part of the plan. Every form on it goes straight to your AI team, so no lead ever waits." Use it to close, not to open — the booked-jobs outcome stays the headline. If their current site is embarrassing, this lands HARD; if their site is fine, skip it.

## Objection handling
- **"How much?"** — Don't lead with price; lead with the booked job. When asked: **founding rate $1,500/mo,
  locked for life** (standard becomes $2,500–5,000), **and you pay nothing until appointments are booked on
  your calendar.** One saved roofing/HVAC job ($10–30k) covers most of a year. Anchor: an agency runs $3–8k/mo
  and an inside sales rep $4–5k/mo loaded — you'd need both to do this. **Never drop the price** — if it's a
  flinch, the value demo was weak; go back to the rebooking number.
- **"So you just send appointments — do I still have to close them?"** — Yes, you close (you're the expert on
  your jobs and pricing). I find and book qualified appointments on your calendar; you do what you already do
  best. You don't pay me until those appointments are landing.
- **"Is this spammy / will it annoy my customers?"** — It only messages people you already have a
  relationship with (past customers, prior quote requests), it's consent-native and compliant
  (TCPA/10DLC handled), and **you approve every message before it sends.** Nothing goes out you didn't
  sign off on.
- **"Is AI really doing this?"** — Don't argue it, show it: "Give me your list and I'll have drafts in
  your voice ready for you to read shortly. You'll see exactly what it'd send before anything goes out."
- **"I don't have time to set this up."** — That's the point: it's done-for-you. I operate it, you approve
  from your phone. Setup is me loading your list — ~15 minutes of your time.
- **"My website is old/ugly / I don't even have one."** — Included: I build you a new one, free, and we
  host + maintain it. It's wired so every lead from it gets answered in under 2 minutes by your AI team.
  (Never quote the website as a separate line item — it's part of the plan.)

## The anchor (one line)
"A human doing this — a rep chasing every old lead — runs $50k+/yr. This is a fraction of that,
month-to-month, and you don't pay until it's working."
