# Outreach swipe file — businesses already running ads (selling Surge)

**The target:** businesses you can verify are actively running ads via the **Meta Ad Library** (facebook.com/ads/library) and **Google Ads Transparency Center**. These are warm B2B prospects — they have budget, they accept that acquisition costs money, and they feel the pain.

**The angle (defensible):** you can see *that* they advertise and *how long*, but NOT how their ads convert. So never pitch "we'll tell you how your ads convert" — they'll know you're bluffing. Pitch the gap you can credibly name and that Surge actually fixes: **leads going cold after the click.** Speed-to-lead is the single biggest leak in small-business marketing, and almost no owner answers fast enough.

**Targeting rule:** prioritize advertisers running the SAME ad 4+ months — committed budget, real pain if leads leak. Long-running ads = your warmest list.

## How to run it (don't get burned)
- **Personalize with the real ad.** Reference the actual creative you saw ("the before/after [service] video"). Generic = ignored; specific = credible.
- **Manual, individual outreach — not bulk harvest-and-blast.** The Ad Library is for research; scraping at scale + mass emailing crosses CAN-SPAM (US) and Meta's terms. B2B to business owners is fine; volume blasting is where people get burned.
- **Include a physical address + unsubscribe** once you scale email (CAN-SPAM) — already built into Surge's sending.
- **A/B the temperature.** Soft pulls more replies; sharp pulls more qualified-but-fewer. Test which these advertisers respond to.
- **Dogfood it:** this is a perfect lead source for our own Aria — find active advertisers, reference their ad, pitch the post-click gap. Surge selling Surge.
