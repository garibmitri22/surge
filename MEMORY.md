## 🧭 B2B vs B2C MOTIONS (Mitri + CEO, June 6 — major reframe, locked)
The current lead engine is COLD OUTBOUND TO BUSINESSES (B2B): research companies + paced cold email. That's why runs surface businesses + even a YouTuber — cold research is inherently B2B. TCPA = **$500–$1,500 per message/call, no cap** (2025–26 settlements: SiriusXM $28M, Kaiser $10.5M). Carriers **block 100% of unregistered automated SMS** since Feb 2025 (10DLC registration mandatory). Texas has stricter-than-federal rules. (Cowork is not a lawyer — counsel before launch.)
- **Residential is won 3 legal ways, all consent-native:** (1) **Speed-to-lead on INBOUND** (answer ad/form leads in <2 min, 24/7 — Aria), (2) **Database reactivation** (the owner's OWN lapsed members/old quotes who already consented — fastest legal revenue, best first demo = the gym friend's member list), (3) reviews/referrals. "People in my area know about it" = AWARENESS = ads + local content = NOVA's lane, not Aria outreach. Don't blur awareness with lead gen.
- **Channels:** SMS (~98% open) + voice DO beat email for B2C — but ONLY to consented contacts (their DB + fresh inbound). With consent SMS is the best channel; without it it's a lawsuit. Infra = Twilio + 10DLC.
- **Two products now:** "Fill my pipeline" (B2B cold — built) AND "Wake up your database + never miss a lead" (B2C inbound + reactivation — consent-native, multi-channel). B2C is arguably bigger/safer/faster-to-revenue and fits gym/realtor/contractor.
