# Surge — Proof-of-Concept Plan (investor-grade)

**The bar:** convince someone about to write a $1M check. At that level they don't buy polish or promises — they probe "is this real?" So the entire proof is built on REAL data and verifiable outcomes, never theater. (Per `NORTH-STAR.md`: real or transparently-estimated, never invented.)

## What actually convinces a serious investor (in order)
1. A **real, end-to-end outcome** on real data — not a scripted demo.
2. A **moat that compounds** (why this isn't a copyable wrapper).
3. **Unit economics** that work at scale.
4. A **repeatable engine** (this wasn't a one-off; it runs every day).

## The proof loop we run NOW (live, real data — this is "the best loop today")
The full Nova→ads top-of-funnel isn't built yet (and we don't fake it). What IS real and demonstrable end-to-end:
1. **Aria finds real businesses** — researches, scores 0–100 with reasons + source URL, on the customer's real ICP. (Proven: a real run produced 16 real Houston→Conroe med-spa leads.)
2. **Aria drafts personalized outreach** with a tracked booking link, owner approves.
3. **It actually sends** — real email on a verified domain (first email sent + inbox-confirmed June 5).
4. **The warm signal fires** — prospect clicks the tracked link → lead auto-promotes to warm → owner pinged. Real action, real signal.
5. **Reactivation** — point Aria at a real contractor's existing customer/quote list → real rebooking conversations → booked jobs. **This is the money shot** — revenue from a list they already own, day one.
6. **The company brain** — show that Aria's work is shaped by stored ICP/voice/SOPs, i.e. it's custom and gets stickier over time.

**The single most convincing artifact = one documented real customer result:** "Surge rebooked 3 jobs from [contractor]'s old-quote list in week one." One real outcome beats any deck.

## The metrics that matter (real, from our own data)
- **Cost per real run: ~$0.33** (Haiku routing) — heavy run ~$1.50–2.
- **Gross margin: ~67–70%** even at full burn (`lib/pricing.mjs`).
- **Price vs. value:** $399 single / $999 team vs. a $50K+/yr human hire = ~93% cheaper. Anchor every number to this.
- **Time-to-first-value:** near-zero (day-one activation — agents work the moment onboarding ends).
- **Funnel:** leads/run → drafts → sends (warmup-capped) → clicks → warm → booked. Show the real counts; estimate pipeline only as (qualified × the customer's avg deal value), labeled.

## The moat narrative (why it's not a wrapper)
The model (Claude) is a commodity everyone can call — so it's NOT the moat. The moat is the wrapping: (1) the **memory layer** = switching cost (3 months of company brain = can't leave); (2) **real actions** through real infrastructure (email/SMS/consent/tracking) — a system of action, not a chat box; (3) **vertical depth**; (4) the **network-learning flywheel** once customers scale. The brain improving lifts everyone; our data is what keeps us ahead.
