# Surge — Unit Economics (the money slide)

Real or clearly-labeled-estimated numbers only (per NORTH-STAR). The investor/customer money story in one page.

## Cost to deliver (COGS)
- **A real Aria run: ~$0.33** (16 real leads + 7 drafts, Haiku routing — actual logged run, June 5).
- A heavy run: **~$1.50–$2.00**.
- Chat with the team: **pennies** (and it's unlimited to the customer — retention, not a cost center).
- The expensive line is video (Nova P3) — metered, not in scope yet.

## Price vs. value
| | Price | = per year | vs. a human hire |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single employee | $399/mo | $4,788/yr | a human costs $50,000+/yr → **~90% cheaper** |
| Team (Aria+Nova+Opus, run by Atlas) | $999/mo | $11,988/yr | replaces an SDR ($4–5k/mo) + an agency ($3–8k/mo) |
| Managed Growth Engine | $2,500–5,000/mo + their ad spend | — | done-for-you; still cheaper than agency + SDR combined |

Always anchor to the $50K human. If price is the objection, the value demo is wrong — never drop the price (CEO.md Pricing Compression Trap).

## Margin
- Hours allowances (Single 70h / Team 200h) are set so a **100%-burned plan still clears ≥~70% gross margin** at plan price (`lib/pricing.mjs`). Most customers never fully burn → margin runs higher.
- Overtime packs ($25→20h, $99→100h) are priced above COGS → margin-accretive, not a discount.

## The path to $1M ARR (illustrative blend)
$1M ARR ≈ $83K/month. Realistic mix:
- ~15 managed @ $3,000 = **$45K/mo**
- ~40 self-serve @ $999 = **$40K/mo**
- ≈ **$85K/mo → ~$1M/yr, ~55 customers.**
Higher-ACV managed tier is the accelerator — fewer logos to reach the number solo.

## Why the margin holds at scale (the moat → LTV)
The model (Claude) is a commodity that keeps getting cheaper/faster — so COGS trends DOWN over time while price holds on value. And the memory layer creates switching cost → low churn → high LTV. Better base models lift everyone; our accumulated customer data is what keeps the gap. Cheaper inputs + sticky customers = expanding margins as we grow.

## The one-line version
"It costs us cents to do a run, we charge for the outcome against a $50K human, we clear ~70%+ margin even redlined, and the memory moat makes customers stay — so margins widen as we scale."
